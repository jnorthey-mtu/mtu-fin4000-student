# FIN 4000 Midterm Study Guide

**Coverage:** BKM Chapters 1–5 and 14–16 · **Updated:** October 6, 2026 · **Instructor:** Jim Northey

## Overview

The midterm will have a set of multiple-choice questions in CFA format and a set of calculations, with at least one calculation from each chapter. The exam is timed so that students who have studied should finish in less than an hour.

- The exam is on paper and taken in class.
- You may use a TI BA II Plus or TI BA II Plus Professional calculator. Smartphone calculator apps are not permitted.
- A practice exam will be available by Monday, October 12.

Each chapter below lists the topics to know, the study guide that covers each one, and the BKM reading, followed by what you should be able to do.
